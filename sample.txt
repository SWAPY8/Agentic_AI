The crisp autumn air bit at his cheeks as Marcus stepped onto the old wooden pier. Beneath him, the dark lake water lapped gently against the mossy pilings, whispering secrets of a time long forgotten. 
He pulled his wool collar tight against the rising wind, his eyes scanning the misty horizon where the gray sky met the tree line.In his coat pocket, his fingers tightly gripped the small brass key. It was the only thing his grandfather had left him, tucked away inside a dusty velvet box with no explanation, no note, and no instructions. 
For months, Marcus had searched for what it unlocked, following a trail of faded postcards and cryptic journal entries that finally led him to this isolated northern shore.Suddenly, a soft chime broke the silence. He pulled out his pocket watch, but the hands were spinning backward in a frantic, silent blur. A sudden tremor shook the wooden planks beneath his boots. Out in the center of the lake, the heavy fog began to swirl, parting slowly to reveal the stark, jagged silhouette of an island that had definitely not been on any map.
